Renting a villa in Lonavala: what to know

Lonavala sits on the Sahyadri hill range between Mumbai and Pune, and villa stays have become the usual way groups visit — a private house with a kitchen and a garden works better for a group of eight or twelve than splitting into hotel rooms. Most Lonavala villas for rent are built on hill plots with a view of the valley, and a good share of them add a private pool for exactly this kind of weekend. Here's a short guide to the areas, seasons and questions people usually have before booking one.

Popular areas to rent a villa

Tiger Point

Perched high on the cliffside, villas here offer dramatic valley views and misty cloud coverage during the monsoons. Perfect for luxury group stays wanting scenic balcony views and proximity to Lion's Point.

Bhushi Dam

One of Lonavala's most iconic locations, set close to the overflowing waterfalls and lush green hills. Ideal for family villas looking for central access to local markets, dining, and monsoon attractions.

Pawna Lake

Villas here sit right along the tranquil backwaters, offering private lake views, expansive lawns, and outdoor BBQ setups — the top pick for peaceful retreats and large gathering private pools.

Bhaje Caves

Nestled near the historic rock-cut caves and Malavli railway station, this area features quiet cottage-style villas and bungalows surrounded by greenery, popular for relaxed family getaways.

Best time to book a Lonavala villa

Monsoon (June – September)

Lonavala's best-known season — waterfalls fill up and the hills turn green, though roads can get slippery, so villas with covered parking and a proper access road are worth checking.

Winter (October – February)

Clear skies, cool evenings and the most comfortable weather for a bonfire deck or an outdoor pool during the day — the busiest season for weekend bookings.

Summer (March – May)

Warmer and quieter, with fewer crowds at the viewpoints — a good time to book a villa with a private pool for a lower rate than peak season.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a villa in Lonavala?+

Villas here typically run ₹15,000 to ₹60,000 per night depending on the area, bedroom count and whether there's a private pool. Smaller 2–3 BHK cottages near Kune or Pawna sit at the lower end; larger pool villas near Tungarli or Khandala cost more.

Which area should I pick for a villa stay?+

Tungarli suits groups who want to be close to town and the main viewpoints. Old Khandala Road is quieter with bigger gardens. Pawna Lake is best for a lakeside stay. Kune Village suits smaller, couple-friendly stays near the waterfall.

Do all the villas have a private pool?+

No — it varies by villa. Use the "Private pool only" filter above the villa grid to see just the pool villas.

How far is Lonavala from Mumbai and Pune?+

About 90 km (roughly 2 hours by road) from Mumbai, and about 65 km (around 1.5 hours) from Pune — which is why it's a common weekend villa destination for both cities.
