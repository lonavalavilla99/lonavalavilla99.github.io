Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a villa in Lonavala?+

Villas here start from around ₹5,000 per night and go up to ₹60,000, depending on the area, bedroom count and whether there's a private pool. Smaller cottages sit at the lower end; larger pool villas near Tungarli or Khandala cost more.

Which area should I pick for a villa stay?+

Tungarli suits groups who want to be close to town and the main viewpoints. Old Khandala Road is quieter with bigger gardens. Pawna Lake is best for a lakeside stay. Kune Village suits smaller, couple-friendly stays near the waterfall.

Do all the villas have a private pool?+

No — it varies by villa. Use the "Private pool only" filter above the villa grid to see just the pool villas.

How far is Lonavala from Mumbai and Pune?+

About 90 km (roughly 2 hours by road) from Mumbai, and about 65 km (around 1.5 hours) from Pune — which is why it's a common weekend villa destination for both cities.

How does booking actually work?+

Filter for what you need, then tap "Book on WhatsApp" on a villa card. It opens WhatsApp with that villa's name, location, BHK and price already filled in — you confirm dates and payment directly with the host from there.

What happens after I make the payment?+

After you make the payment, you'll get a booking confirmation on WhatsApp — including guest details, villa details and staff details — for smooth coordination during your stay.
